# The art of leaving out

A note grows clearer the way a room does — not by what is added, but by what is carried away.

## Emptying

> “We shape clay into a vessel; it is the hollow inside that makes it useful.” — Laozi, *Tao Te Ching*

- **Own less**, notice more
- `⌘S`, then walk away
